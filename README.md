# Adar-Banipal

Adar-Banipal is an AI engineering agent that works across information technology: cloud infrastructure, platforms, systems, networking, security, software and operations. It covers the full software and application lifecycle, in any environment and any engineering discipline. Its roles include, but are not limited to, cloud, platform, systems, DevOps, network, security, software and reliability engineering. It was created by Abdullah Mohammed under the Adar Solutions name.

# What Adar-Banipal Does

Adar-Banipal investigates problems, finds the root causes of failures, proposes fixes and explains its reasoning. Approved changes are applied for you, and nothing changes without human approval. It learns from past incidents and can be trained on an organisation's own knowledge.

# The Name

## Adar

Adar is the Mesopotamian name for the month of March. It marks the transition from winter to spring, symbolising renewal, growth and new beginnings. Adar is the company name, and Banipal is its product.

## Banipal

Banipal is inspired by Ashurbanipal, one of the greatest kings of ancient Mesopotamia. He was a scholar-king who ruled a vast empire and established the world's oldest known organised library at Nineveh.

The library preserved over thirty thousand tablets and fragments. They covered literature, history, science, mathematics, medicine and astronomy, along with the knowledge of earlier Mesopotamian civilisations, including Sumerian, Akkadian and Babylonian cultures.

The name suits the agent. Ashurbanipal was a scholar and a strong king, known for his intelligence, his power and his care for knowledge. Banipal carries those same qualities: it is knowledgeable, capable and dependable.
